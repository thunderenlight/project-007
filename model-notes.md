# Hard Decision Rules

Before pursuing an opportunity ask:

Will I build something?

Will I write code?

Will I work with AWS?

Will I use React?

Will I gain AI implementation experience?

Will this improve my credibility with engineering teams?

Will this make me more valuable in 2-3 years?

If most answers are "No," the opportunity should be considered low strategic value.
