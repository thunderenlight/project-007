## Raw Description

As an Application Support Engineer specializing in Front End Development, you will play a critical role in maintaining, enhancing, and evolving enterprise-scale web applications. You will combine deep technical expertise with strong problem-solving skills to diagnose complex issues, improve application performance, and deliver exceptional user experiences across business-critical systems.

In this role, you will leverage advanced React.js and JavaScript expertise to support and modernize front-end platforms built on micro-repository and distributed application architectures. You will collaborate closely with product owners, designers, architects, and backend engineering teams to implement scalable solutions, optimize system reliability, and drive continuous improvement initiatives.

Key responsibilities include:
- Troubleshoot and resolve complex front-end application issues across multiple products and platforms.
- Design, develop, and support React-based solutions within micro-repository and modular application architectures.
- Build and maintain reusable component libraries, shared design systems, and front-end frameworks that accelerate development across teams.
- Develop intuitive and accessible user experiences that align with modern UI/UX best practices and enterprise design standards.
- Create and enhance data visualization solutions using modern front-end technologies to transform complex business data into actionable insights.
- Improve application performance, scalability, observability, and maintainability through engineering best practices and automation.
- Partner with cross-functional teams to define technical solutions, influence architectural decisions, and ensure successful delivery of business objectives.
- Establish and document front-end development standards, patterns, and best practices to promote consistency across applications.
- Mentor developers and provide technical guidance to enable adoption of modern front-end technologies and engineering practices.
- Act as a technical leader, coordinating efforts across multiple teams and helping to drive strategic front-end initiatives.

**Growth and Leadership Opportunities:**
- Influence front-end architecture strategy and modernization efforts.
- Lead adoption of React, micro-frontend, and micro-repository development patterns.
- Shape enterprise design systems and component governance models.
- Mentor and upskill engineers across multiple teams.
- Guide the implementation of advanced data visualization and analytics experiences.
- Collaborate with engineering resources, architects, UX specialists, and product teams to deliver high-impact solutions at scale.

**Required Skills:**
- Advanced React.js development experience
- Advanced JavaScript and modern front-end engineering practices
- Experience with micro-repository, micro-frontend, or distributed front-end architectures
- Strong understanding of UI/UX principles and accessibility standards
- Experience building and supporting data visualization solutions
- Proven ability to troubleshoot and resolve complex production issues
