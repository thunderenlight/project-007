### AI Product Manager

**What the role actually is:**
Owns the roadmap and strategy for an AI-powered product. Works directly with engineers to define features, sets evaluation criteria for AI quality, and makes tradeoffs between model capability and user experience. This is a *product* role with deep AI literacy requirements — not a project manager with "AI" in the title.

**What it is NOT:**
- A PM who coordinates AI initiatives without technical involvement
- A governance role renamed "AI PM"
- A delivery manager on an AI project

**How to tell the difference:**
Ask: "Will I define which model is used, design the evaluation framework, and own the prompt architecture?" If no — it is not a true AI PM role.

**Required skills:**
- LLM fundamentals (what models can and cannot do, tradeoffs)
- Prompt engineering and evaluation
- Product management (roadmap, backlog, prioritization, user stories)
- API literacy (can read a Swagger doc, understand JSON payloads)
- Familiarity with cloud deployment (where the AI runs)
- Data analysis (can interpret model performance metrics)

**Differentiating skills:**
- Hands-on Claude / Bedrock / OpenAI API experience
- RAG architecture understanding
- LangChain or LangGraph exposure
- Python (enough to prototype)

**How to position:**
Lead with AI Governance and Enablement experience. Bridge to product ownership via the Amethyst Studio work. Show technical literacy through this project. The narrative: "I have seen AI governance from the inside — now I want to own the product."

**Target companies / engagement types:**
- Fintech clients with AI-powered document or decisioning products
- Healthcare clients with clinical AI applications
- Internal Accenture AI platform teams
- Technology companies with B2B AI products

**Compensation signal:**
$140K–$200K+ at director level, depending on company and industry.

## Role Red Flags

These titles often appear in the market but frequently describe anti-pattern roles. Evaluate carefully before pursuing:

| Title | Common Trap | How to Verify |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| "AI Program Manager" | Often PMO coordination, not product | Ask: "Will I write requirements or manage timelines?" |
| "AI Change Manager" | Pure change management, no technical work | Ask: "Will I work with engineers and data scientists?" |
| "Digital Transformation Lead" | Broad, often no AI or cloud | Ask: "What specific AI or cloud technologies will I work with?" |
| "AI Governance Lead" | Can be rich or repetitive | Ask: "Will I build governance tools or write governance documents?" |
| "Innovation Consultant" | Often undefined and unfocused | Ask: "What will I have shipped at the end of this engagement?" |
