## Internal Accenture Targets

### Accenture AI Practice / Applied Intelligence

**Why attractive:** Largest addressable opportunity — already employed, network exists, internal roles require less formal application process.

**Target teams:**
- Applied Intelligence practice leads
- Cloud First AI teams
- Amethyst / internal AI platform team
- GenAI COE (Center of Excellence)

**How to pursue:**
Internal networking over formal applications. Identify the right people directly. Express interest in AI-forward engagements at performance review cycles.

**Key signal:** Engagements with clients who are building AI products, not just implementing off-the-shelf AI tools.

## Financial Services Clients

Financial services is one of the highest-demand sectors for AI PMs. Document-heavy, compliance-aware, and actively investing in AI for decisioning, document processing, and customer service.

---

## Healthcare / Life Sciences Clients

Clinical AI, health record summarization, and diagnostic support are active investment areas. Strong overlap with AI governance background (FDA, HIPAA considerations reward governance experience).

## Technology Companies

Add specific companies as you research them. Technology companies offer the strongest technical growth signal but often require demonstrated engineering skills.

## How to Research Accounts

1. **LinkedIn:** Search "[Company] AI Product Manager" to see recent hires and job postings
2. **Company blog / engineering blog:** How technical is their AI work?
3. **Job postings:** What stack do they use? What are they building?
4. **Press releases and news:** Recent AI product launches
5. **AWS/Anthropic partner announcements:** Are they listed as a strategic partner?

When you find something valuable, add it to the relevant account profile here.
